Advanced driving standards

Independently tested advanced driving skills within our team

Yalding Taxis owner Michael Stead is an IAM RoadSmart Master Driver. IAM RoadSmart describes its Masters standard as the ultimate challenge for civilian drivers and says the programme is designed to attain the highest standard of civilian driving ability in the country.

Our team also includes an IAM RoadSmart Advanced Driver, bringing independently tested advanced driving skills into the business alongside our local knowledge and professional taxi experience.

Clear and transparent: IAM RoadSmart Masters or Advanced status is held by selected Yalding Taxis drivers. An IAM-qualified driver is not guaranteed on every booking.
